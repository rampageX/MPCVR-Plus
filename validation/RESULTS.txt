To reproduce portable tests from the extracted package, with the repository's
existing Source/OsdPlus/AudioDepth.h on the include path:

g++ -std=c++20 -Wall -Wextra -Werror -pedantic -fsanitize=address,undefined \
    -I /path/to/MPCVR-Plus/Source/OsdPlus validation/AudioFormatsTests.cpp \
    -o AudioFormatsTests
./AudioFormatsTests

The validation folder is not required for installation or playback.
